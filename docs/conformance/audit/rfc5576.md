# RFC 5576: Source-Specific Media Attributes in SDP: clause audit

Spec: RFC 5576, June 2009. Audited at fork master 8916bc3. Scope: `a=ssrc`, `a=ssrc-group`, the `cname`,
`previous-ssrc` and `fmtp` source attributes, FID / FEC source groups (FEC-FR is RFC 5956), offer/answer
use (sections 8 and 9) and the grammar of section 11. IANA registration rules (12) bind authors of new
attributes, not the engine.

What the engine writes for a non-simulcast sender (`with_media_source`, `rtci-sdp/src/description/media.rs:203`):
`a=ssrc-group:FID <ssrc> <rtx>`, `a=ssrc-group:FEC-FR <ssrc> <fec>`, then for each SSRC
`a=ssrc:<n> cname:<stream id>`, `a=ssrc:<n> msid:<stream id> <track id>` (and, until the rtci-sdp
conformance work, `mslabel` and `label`). Simulcast senders write no `a=ssrc` lines unless
`write_ssrc_attributes_for_simulcast` is set.

Path legend: `sdp/mod.rs` = `src/peer_connection/sdp/mod.rs`, `media.rs` = `rtci-sdp/src/description/media.rs`,
`icpt` = `src/peer_connection/handler/interceptor.rs`, `pc/` = `src/peer_connection/`.

## Gaps, in order of importance

1. **SSRC uniqueness and collisions (M).** RFC5576-5-1, 5-3, 5-7, 8-1, 8-2, 5-9, 6.1-2, 5-8, 6.2-1: draw
   local SSRCs that are not in the remote description or already used locally; detect a collision
   (remote packet or SDES with our SSRC); change SSRC and renegotiate with `previous-ssrc`.
2. **Wider groups (S).** RFC5576-11-3, 4.2-2, 7.2-1, 4.1-2: accept FID / FEC-FR groups of more than
   two ids, accept `FEC`, detect one SSRC declared in two bundled sections. (The remote cname is kept
   per source since cname-1, RFC5576-6.1-2.)
3. ~~Stop writing unregistered source attributes.~~ Done: RFC5576-4.1-6.
4. **Undeclared sources next to declared ones (S).** RFC5576-5-6: what remains is an extra
   undeclared SSRC in a section that declares several streams (`SIM`).
5. **Tests.** RFC5576-6.1-1 (SDP cname equals the SDES CNAME of a sender), 5-2.

## Bugs found

- **RFC5576-4.1-6 (fixed):** every non-simulcast sender wrote `a=ssrc:<n> mslabel:...` and
  `a=ssrc:<n> label:...` (`rtci-sdp/src/description/media.rs`, `with_media_source`). Test:
  `rtci-sdp audit_bugs_test.rs::media_source_writes_only_registered_source_attributes`.
- **RFC5576-5-3 / 8-1:** local SSRCs are plain `rand::random::<u32>()` (`src/peer_connection/internal.rs:2183`):
  nothing prevents two encodings of one connection, an encoding and its RTX flow, or a local and a
  remote source from sharing an SSRC, and nothing detects it afterwards. Reproduce (deterministic):
  give two senders explicit `sendEncodings` with the same `ssrc`; both are accepted and written into
  one BUNDLE group.
